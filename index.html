Privacy Policy — Ichha

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Ichha is developed by Karnata Prayogalaya ("we," "us," "our"). This policy explains what data Ichha collects, how it's used, and your rights regarding it.

What we collect

  • Account information: email address (and/or phone number), used for authentication via Supabase.
  • App content you create: journal entries, daily intentions, emotional reflections, SWOT reviews, life-area notes, and related personal reflection data you enter into the app.
  • Basic usage data: app settings and sync timestamps needed for the app to function offline and sync across sessions.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your data to third parties.
  • We do not use your journal or reflection content for advertising.
  • We do not display third-party ads in the app.
  • We do not share your personal content with other users — any future social/sharing features will be strictly opt-in, per item, and will never include raw journal entries, emotion logs, or private reflections.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored using Supabase (PostgreSQL database and authentication service), hosted on infrastructure located in India. A local cache (SQLite) is also kept on your device to allow offline use, syncing to the server when you're back online.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at avinashzoom@gmail.com. Deleting your account will permanently remove your journal entries, reflections, and associated data from our servers.

Children's use

Ichha is not directed at children and is not designed for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Health & wellness content

Ichha includes educational reference content (e.g., a Nutrition Handbook) for informational purposes only. This content is not medical advice, and Ichha is not a substitute for professional medical, psychological, or nutritional consultation.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
