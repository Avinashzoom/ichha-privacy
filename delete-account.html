Delete Your Ichha Account

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Ichha (developed by Karnata Prayogalaya) allows you to request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

How to request deletion

To request that your Ichha account and data be deleted, send an email to avinashzoom@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete My Ichha Account", using the same email address associated with your Ichha account. We will process your request within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account credentials (email/login information)
  • All journal entries, daily intentions, and reflections
  • SWOT reviews and life-area data
  • Any other personal content you created within the app

What may be retained

We do not retain any personal data beyond deletion, except where required by law (e.g., financial records if applicable, which do not currently apply to Ichha as it has no in-app purchases at this time).

Retention period

Deletion requests are processed within 30 days of receipt. Once processed, your data is permanently removed from our active systems.
